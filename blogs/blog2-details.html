Tax Filing: A Beginner’s Guide

Published on January 15, 2024

Tax Filing Basics
Tax filing is an essential part of personal and business finances. Whether you're filing your taxes as a freelancer or running a corporation, understanding the tax filing process is crucial.

Types of Taxes

  • Income Tax: Paid on earned income.
  • Corporate Tax: Paid by businesses based on their profits.
  • Sales Tax: Paid on goods and services sold.
  • Property Tax: Paid based on property ownership.

Documents You Need

  • Income statements (e.g., W-2 or 1099 forms in the U.S.)
  • Receipts for business expenses
  • Bank statements
  • Investment income documents
  • Tax deductions and credits records

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Failing to report all income sources.
  • Missing out on potential deductions or credits.
  • Filing after the deadline.
  • Incorrectly reporting business expenses.
